Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What information do I need to provide? +

Basic property and lighting information is enough to start. You can also upload relevant plans, photos or reports.

Do I need to know how many lights I have? +

No. Provide whatever information you have available and the team can review the opportunity from there.

Can I send photos or existing plans? +

Yes. The enquiry form allows you to upload relevant plans, photos and reports.

Does LED always produce the biggest saving? +

Not necessarily. Lighting controls, occupancy patterns, dimming and operating hours can also affect the opportunity.
